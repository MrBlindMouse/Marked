# Marked

Marked adds your marks to your memories.

Add a logo, signature, text, sticker or timestamp to a photo or video — right on your device. No cloud, no upload, no account. A mark should look like it was always part of the media.

## What can you mark?

- **Logos** — PNG assets or built-in SVG vector marks
- **Text** — custom copy with size, font (Sans / Serif / Mono), weight, tracking, color, outline, pill, shadow and opacity controls
- **Signatures** — freehand strokes with color, width and opacity
- **Stickers** — emoji with size and opacity controls
- **Timestamps** — `HH:MM:SS`, `HH:MM` or `MM:SS` presets placed at any corner of the media

## How it works

1. Pick a photo or video.
2. Add marks via the **Add Mark** menu (+), or load marks from a saved recipe.
3. Drag to position and pinch to scale; undo, delete or reorder layers.
4. Hit **Process** — the marked photo is saved to your gallery and shared via the share sheet; marked video is encoded frame-by-frame and exported.

## The look

Marked uses an E-Ink-inspired reading aesthetic: warm paper tones, graphite ink and hairline greys, serif/sans type with mono micro-labels. No accent hues — ink is ink, paper is paper.

## Privacy

Marked runs fully on-device. Media and marks are never uploaded anywhere.
